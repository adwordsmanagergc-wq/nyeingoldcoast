The best New Year's Eve parties on the Gold Coast 2026

From Level 77 of Q1 to the beach bars of Burleigh and Kirra, these are the best NYE parties on the Gold Coast. Surfers Paradise has the most choice within walking distance of the midnight fireworks, while Burleigh and Mermaid Beach lean to rooftops and beach clubs.

Booking tips

  • First-release tickets are the cheapest, so buy early.
  • Check whether drinks are included or pay as you go.
  • Most parties are 18+, and Surfers Paradise and Broadbeach venues scan ID late at night.
  • If the fireworks matter, pick a venue near Surfers Paradise, Broadbeach or Coolangatta.
