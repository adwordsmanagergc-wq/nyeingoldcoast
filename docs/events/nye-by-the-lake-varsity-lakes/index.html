New Year's Eve by the Lake

Fireworks reflected in Lake Orr, with live music and food trucks, made for families who want an early night.

What's included

  • Live music
  • Food trucks
  • Kids entertainment
  • 8:30pm fireworks finale over the lake

About New Year's Eve by the Lake

New Year's Eve by the Lake is at Varsity Lakes waterfront, Lake Orr in Varsity Lakes (Robina & Varsity Lakes). It's one of the fireworks events, free, family-friendly options for New Year's Eve 2026 on the Gold Coast. Fireworks: 8:30pm. Age: All ages.
