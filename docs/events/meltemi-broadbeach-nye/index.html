About Meltemi NYE: Sunset on the Aegean & Aegean After Dark

Meltemi NYE: Sunset on the Aegean & Aegean After Dark is at Meltemi Mediterrania in Broadbeach (Broadbeach & Mermaid Beach). It's one of the dinners, family-friendly, under $200 options for New Year's Eve 2026 on the Gold Coast. Fireworks: 8pm & midnight nearby (Kurrawa Park). Age: All ages.
