Nineteen at The Star NYE Dinner

A four-course dinner on The Star's rooftop, with the early sitting timed for the 8pm fireworks and the late sitting for midnight.

What's included

  • 4-course dinner
  • Rooftop restaurant, 19 floors up
  • Panoramic coast and hinterland views

About Nineteen at The Star NYE Dinner

Nineteen at The Star NYE Dinner is at Nineteen at The Star, Level 19, The Darling in Broadbeach (Broadbeach & Mermaid Beach). It's one of the dinners, fine dining, rooftops options for New Year's Eve 2026 on the Gold Coast. Fireworks: 8pm & midnight (elevated views). Age: Check with venue.
