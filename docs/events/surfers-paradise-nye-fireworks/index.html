Surfers Paradise Foreshore NYE Fireworks

The Gold Coast's biggest free NYE gathering: two fireworks shows over the ocean from the beach in front of the Surfers Paradise sign.

What's included

  • Free family fireworks over the ocean at 8pm
  • Midnight fireworks to welcome 2027
  • Cavill Ave and Orchid Ave pedestrian zones (recent years)
  • Restaurants and bars a short walk from the sand
  • G:link at Cypress Ave, Cavill Ave and Surfers Paradise stations

About Surfers Paradise Foreshore NYE Fireworks

Surfers Paradise Foreshore NYE Fireworks is at Surfers Paradise Beach & Esplanade in Surfers Paradise (Surfers Paradise). It's one of the fireworks events, free, family-friendly, beachfront options for New Year's Eve 2026 on the Gold Coast. Fireworks: 8pm & midnight. Age: All ages.
