Gold Coast New Year's Eve cruises 2026

The Broadwater is calm, sheltered and close to the fireworks, which makes a cruise one of the best ways to see in the new year on the Gold Coast. You can choose an early family cruise for the 8pm show or an adults-only party boat with drinks and canapés for midnight.

Booking tips

  • Choose your show: family cruises are timed for 8pm, party cruises for midnight.
  • Check where you board. Departures are from Southport, Surfers Paradise and Main Beach.
  • Most midnight cruises are 18+ and need photo ID.
  • Cruises sell out. YOT Club's 2025 White Party sold out before the night.
