New Year's Eve dinners on the Gold Coast 2026

A table with a view of the ocean or the Broadwater is the most relaxed way to see in the new year on the Gold Coast. Early sittings usually finish in time for the 8pm fireworks and suit families, while late sittings come with drinks packages that run past midnight.

Booking tips

  • Book early. Beachfront tables at Burleigh and Main Beach sell out by December.
  • Early sittings ($99–$220 in 2025) are the best value and often have child pricing.
  • Check whether the restaurant can actually see a fireworks display. Burleigh had no council show in 2025.
  • Use the free late-night public transport (offered in 2025) rather than driving.
